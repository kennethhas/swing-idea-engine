Two facts dominate the scan and matter more than any single idea:

1. **Almost no live supply zones overhead.** Across 45 names, price has closed through
   essentially all prior supply. Only SLB had a genuine zone-derived T1. Every other target
   is a swing-high or ATR-measured objective and is labelled as an estimate.
2. **20 of 45 names have no live demand zone within 15% of spot.** They have run far from
   their last base — including MSFT, META, AMZN, TSLA, CAT, MU, AMD, PLD, WMT, PG.

This is a late-extension tape: constructive to hold, poor for fresh zone-based entries.
The honest consequence is that **only 8 of 45 names cleared the gates, in 3 of 11 sectors.**
The empty sectors are a finding, not a gap to be padded.

## Method notes

- The bundled `zone_scanner.py` default (1y window, `--leg-mult 1.6`) found almost no bases
  in this tape — MSFT returned zero zones. Per the skill's own guidance, the scan was widened
  to `{6mo, 1y} × leg_mult {1.6, 1.3, 1.1}`; every zone records the pass that produced it.
  Looser passes detect weaker imbalances, which is part of why so many names scored below 6/9.
- Big Picture / Arrival / Curve enhancers are first-pass heuristics and still need visual
  confirmation on a chart before risking capital.
- Weekly context is derived by resampling the daily series (the container cannot reach the
  weekly feed directly), so weekly zones are coarser than a native weekly scan.
